How it runs

  • Testing — pick dates, Start loads full candles once, paper-replays both indices.
  • Live — Start auto-polls; works only 09:15–15:30; after hours use Testing.
  • Kite 5m limit — max {{ snapshot().kiteStats.maxDaysPerCall }} calendar days per historical call; Trade Desk auto-chunks longer Testing ranges and only refreshes today during Live polls.
  • ATM weekly CE on BUY / PE on SELL · 1 lot · exits follow Historical Tester (SL cap 20, 1.5R, EMA-20, 15:15).
  • Both indices may hold a paper trade at once · real Kite orders later.
