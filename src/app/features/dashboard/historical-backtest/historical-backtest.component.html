Connect Kite on Token first. A full Nifty 500 5-minute pull can take a few minutes. Keep the range to 60 calendar days. History is never invented — missing candles are listed, not filled.

Entry and exit

{{ deskLabel() }}. Times are IST. 15-minute range from 5-minute Kite candles. No live orders.

First 15 minutes

  • Mark 09:15–09:30 high and low.
  • A later 15-minute candle must fully close above that high or below that low.
  • A wick through the level without a close beyond it does not count.

Then 5-minute entry

  • Uptrend: after two red 5-minute candles (two lows), the next green candle’s high is entry and its low is SL.
  • Downtrend: after two green 5-minute candles, the next red candle’s low is entry and its high is SL.
  • Last new entry 14:30. Buy/sell stop fills on a later 5-minute bar. Risk ~0.75% of capital (~₹75 on ₹10k). Notional cap 5× capital.

Exit and print

  • No automatic target — you decide from the 30-minute marks.
  • SL hit closes the whole trade. Leftover positions exit at the 15:15 close (EOD).
  • Max amount went is the best rupee move after fill, even if SL later takes it.

Desk selection

  • {{ crowdLabel(crowdFilter) }} — 09:45 full closes only, knowable before most entries.
  • Then take {{ rankLabel(rankBy) }}. Winner desk uses 5 names on ₹10k; research cap is 8.
  • Same first-15 two-low pattern. Scale quantity with capital, not the name count.
