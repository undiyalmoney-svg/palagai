Nifty 500 opening-range book

Opening range 09:15–09:45 IST. Entry is a 5-minute close through the range between 09:50 and 14:30. Stop is 1R. Target is configurable. Time exit 14:50. Uses the same ORB rules as the existing stock script — no extra filters.

Connect Kite on Token first. A full Nifty 500 5-minute pull can take a few minutes. Keep the range to 60 calendar days. History is never invented — missing candles are listed, not filled.

Entry and exit

Same stock ORB script. Target R is the only setting you change here. Times are IST, 5-minute bars.

Entry

  • Opening range is 09:15–09:45 high / low (ORH / ORL).
  • Long: a 09:50–14:30 bar closes through ORH (close > ORH and open ≤ ORH).
  • Short: that bar closes through ORL (close < ORL and open ≥ ORL).
  • Fill is the close plus 0.05% slippage. Risk is ~0.75% of starting capital (₹300 on ₹40,000). Notional cap is 5× capital.

Exit (first hit; same-bar stop before target)

  • Stop is 1R. Target is Target R × R (default 2). Time exit is the 14:50 close.
  • R = clamp(opening-range width × 0.5, 0.30%, 0.60%) of price.
  • Why column: SL, TARGET, or TIME. Approximate Zerodha MIS charges. No live orders.

When a cross is not a desk trade

  • Open gap > 2%, or OR width outside 0.20%–3.00% of prior close.
  • Breakout volume < 1.2× OR average, or close-break < 0.04% past ORH/ORL.
  • Close not on the EMA20 side (long needs close > EMA20).
  • Nifty 50 below VWAP blocks longs (and above VWAP blocks shorts) when those candles load.
  • Desk cap (max stocks / day, default 3) and 15-minute cooldown after an exit.
