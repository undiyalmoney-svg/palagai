How it runs

  • Sessions — Morning 10:30–12:00 ORB (≤1/day) and Evening 19:00–21:00 PDHL (≤2/day). Both on by default for Testing & Live. No entries outside these windows. SL 80 · TP 200 · day stop shared.
  • Hold — until TP/SL, or force-flat at 23:10. Window end only stops new entries.
  • Testing — From/To dates + Result days (Mon–Fri) filter · Download PDF for profits.
  • Live paper — auto-poll during MCX hours; after hours use Testing.
  • Live money — tick the box + confirm; MCX NRML MARKET entry + protective SL-M.
  • PDF — Download PDF for totals, best/worst days, weekday rollup, and trades.
  • ATM CRUDEOILM CE on BUY / PE on SELL · Option ₹ / order qty scales with Lots · Nifty/Bank desk separate.
