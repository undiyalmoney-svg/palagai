How it runs

  • Daily Income (default) — same ORB + PDHL entries, tighter SL/TP for ~₹300–₹1,000/day on 1 lot (₹10/pt): Morning SL40/TP80 · Evening SL40/TP50 · day profit lock +₹1,000 · day stop −₹500 (strict −₹800). Switch Strategy to Champion for hunt-sized SL80/TP250|150 (no profit lock).
  • Live without real money — open Live tab, leave “Live money” unchecked, press Start. Paper fills only.
  • Sessions — Morning 10:00–12:00 ORB (≤1/day) and Evening 18:30–20:30 PDHL (≤1/day). Both on by default.
  • Hold — until TP/SL, or force-flat at 23:10. Window end only stops new entries.
  • Testing — From/To dates + Result days filter · Download PDF for profits.
  • Live money — tick the box + confirm; MCX MIS MARKET entry + protective SL-M.
  • ATM CRUDEOILM CE on BUY / PE on SELL · Option ₹ / order qty scales with Lots · Nifty/Bank desk separate.
