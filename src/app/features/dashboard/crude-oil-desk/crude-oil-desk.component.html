How it runs

  • Strategy — previous-day high/low break 19:00–21:00 IST; SL 80 · TP 200; exit by 23:10; ≤2/day · ≤8/month.
  • Hold — until TP/SL, or up to ~4 hours (19:00 → 23:10).
  • Testing — default last 14 days; Start paper-replays anytime.
  • Live paper — auto-poll during MCX hours; after hours use Testing.
  • Live money — tick the box + confirm; MCX NRML MARKET entry + protective SL-M.
  • ATM CRUDEOILM CE on BUY / PE on SELL · Option ₹ / order qty scales with Lots · Nifty/Bank desk separate.
