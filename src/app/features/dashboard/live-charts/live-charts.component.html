The pink / teal box is a 1:1 measured move on a closed candle that broke a support or resistance band. Pink is the stop (the swing against the break), teal is the equal-height target. Labels mark Entry at the wall, SL at the far pink edge, and Exit at the far teal edge. It updates as live candles arrive and does not wait for Trade Bot.

BUY / SELL arrows mark where a closed candle turned at, or closed through, one of the support and resistance bands drawn above. They wait for the bar to finish so they do not flip while it is still forming. They are chart reading aids — the live desk decides its own trades and does not act on them.

The percentage on each arrow is setup quality, not a win rate. It scores how textbook the bar looks against the level — how well tested the band was, how strongly the candle closed, how far it cleared or recovered the band, its size against ATR, and volume where the instrument reports any (indices do not). Nothing in it is measured against what happened next, so an 85% is a cleaner-looking setup than a 45%, not a likelier profit. Faint arrows are the low-scoring ones; hover a marked candle to see the breakdown behind its score.

Buy ATM CE / PE places a real market order for the front-expiry at-the-money contract, sized from Kite funds the same way Trade Bot is (₹40,000 per index lot; Crude 3×). Use +/− on a card or in full view to change that lot count for the next order. It runs outside the Trade Bot: no premium cap, no daily loss stop. Manual Buy / Sell still has no stop — you manage that exit. Crude / Nifty / Bank Nifty auto bot each buy the CE or PE the 1:1 box names when that bar closes, then rest an SL — only on today's live session. A past date on the calendar is a paper test (start and end are the same day) and the profit strip shows Crude Oil, Nifty, Bank Nifty and the total. Each manual press still shows the exact contract, its live premium and the rupee cost before anything is sent. The same Buy / Sell buttons sit in full chart view.
