BUY / SELL arrows mark where a candle turned at, or closed through, one of the support and resistance bands drawn above. They are chart reading aids — the live desk decides its own trades and does not act on them.

Buy ATM CE / PE places a real market order for the front-expiry at-the-money contract, at the lots set in Settings. It runs outside the Trade Bot: no premium cap, no daily loss stop, and no stop-loss is attached, so you manage the exit yourself. Each press shows the exact contract, its live premium and the rupee cost before anything is sent.
