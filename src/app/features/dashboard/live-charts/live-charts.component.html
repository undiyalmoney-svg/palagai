1-minute pathway. Every book opens on the 1-minute tape with a 5-minute trend filter. Day High / Day Low mark the session, the last FVG is a compact box, the last BOS is labelled, swept liquidity before that BOS is the HTF IDM (5M-IDM on the default book), SL / PE / TG are the red / yellow / green plan, and Indicator zone-1 / zone-2 sit 15 and 30 minutes after the break. Turn other SMC layers on in settings if you want the heavier overlay back.

How a signal is built. The higher-timeframe trend (bullish, bearish or sideways, from confirmed structure only) sets the allowed direction. Price then has to reach a point of interest — discount / premium, an active order block, a fair value gap or swept liquidity — react, and a BOS or CHoCH in the trade direction must be confirmed by a candle closing beyond the broken level. Stops sit beyond the swing or order block; TP1, TP2 and the final target follow the risk-reward setting (default 1 : 2 minimum). After TP1 the stop moves to break-even, after TP2 to TP1.

No repainting. Only closed candles are analysed. Swings are marked a few bars after they form, structure breaks on a candle close, and past trend values are never rewritten. A setup building on the candle still forming is drawn dashed as “SETUP — NOT CONFIRMED” and cannot become a BUY or SELL until that candle closes. An adverse wick or a single temporary pullback does not exit a trade — only the stop, a target, an opposite confirmed structure break, a higher-timeframe reversal or an invalidated order block does.

Statistics. Backtest and live results are kept apart: live is trades entered during today's session, backtest is everything before it, and a replayed date is all backtest. Results are in R-multiples of the planned risk with no look-ahead.

Buy / Sell / Auto place a real market order for the front-expiry at-the-money contract on every book — Nifty 50, Bank Nifty and Crude. Sized from Kite funds (₹40,000 per index lot; Crude 3×). Auto buys the ATM call on a confirmed BUY and the ATM put on a confirmed SELL, then rests a 25% premium stop and a 0.5R target unless that chart's max profit / max loss is set on this Charts tab — then those rupee caps rest instead. Editing the amount while a fill is open moves that fill's stop and target; if the fill has already crossed the new amount, the stop is cancelled and the contract is sold. Any rupee figure is allowed. Caps are per trade, not a day stop. Auto on Nifty does not arm Bank Nifty or Crude. Manual Buy / Sell ask before sending; Auto does not, because you already armed that book. Open fills, the selected contract, the stop and the target sit on the Live trades board above the charts.
