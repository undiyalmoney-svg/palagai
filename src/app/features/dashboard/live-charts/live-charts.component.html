BUY / SELL arrows mark where a candle turned at, or closed through, one of the support and resistance bands drawn above. They are chart reading aids — the live desk decides its own trades and does not act on them.

The percentage on each arrow is setup quality, not a win rate. It scores how textbook the bar looks against the level — how well tested the band was, how strongly the candle closed, how far it cleared or recovered the band, its size against ATR, and volume where the instrument reports any (indices do not). Nothing in it is measured against what happened next, so an 85% is a cleaner-looking setup than a 45%, not a likelier profit. Faint arrows are the low-scoring ones; hover a marked candle to see the breakdown behind its score.

Buy ATM CE / PE places a real market order for the front-expiry at-the-money contract, sized from Kite funds the same way Trade Bot is (₹40,000 per index lot; Crude 3×). It runs outside the Trade Bot: no premium cap, no daily loss stop, and no stop-loss is attached, so you manage the exit yourself. Each press shows the exact contract, its live premium and the rupee cost before anything is sent. The same Buy / Sell buttons sit in full chart view.
